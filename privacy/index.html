Privacy

Updated 10 October 2026

This is a personal site. The public home page has no analytics, no tracking scripts, no ads and no third-party cookies.

Sign in with Google

The private sections of this site are for its owner only. Signing in with Google asks for one thing, your email address, and only to check that it is the owner's. No other Google data is requested, and the email address is not stored or shared.

A successful sign-in sets a single session cookie on this site so you stay signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes it.

Server logs

Like most web servers, this one keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser, page requested and time) to run and secure the site. They are not used for tracking or shared with anyone.
