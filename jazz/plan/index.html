Start transcribing Easy to Love

Learn a short phrase from the Reddit performance. Its poster identifies the source as Easy to Love from Cannonball Adderley’s Nippon Soul.

  1. Listen, sing, and find one short phrase on the trumpet.
  2. Write it on manuscript paper, then enter the same phrase in MuseScore Studio. Compare rhythm, rests, accidentals, ties, and beaming; correct the handwritten version.
  3. Play along slowly, matching phrasing and articulation. Record a take and save the next timestamp to learn.

The first session is in September 10’s practice plan, after the listening break. Finishing the entire solo is not a prerequisite for continuing Blue Bossa.
