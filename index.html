What you just watched

My name, written as a function on a sphere — zero everywhere except inside the letters — and reconstructed from its spherical-harmonic expansion. As you scrolled, the partial sum swept from ℓ = 0 to ℓ = 90: first the monopole blob, then the low-order modes that only know “something is near the equator,” and finally enough bandwidth to spell. The shimmer around the letters is Gibbs ringing — the honest cost of building a discontinuity out of smooth waves.

Every value is computed in the page: 8,281 coefficients projected with Gauss–Legendre quadrature in Python, re-synthesized in JavaScript with a stable Legendre recurrence, and self-checked against SciPy to ~10−13 on load. The dots are plain DOM elements on a CSS-3D sphere. No canvas, no WebGL.
